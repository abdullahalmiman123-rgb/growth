# Standing Instructions

These rules apply to every task in this repository, for every session, without exception.

## Quality bar

- Deliver the most professional outcome possible. Nothing casual, sloppy, or half-finished ships.
- No mistakes. Verify facts, numbers, file contents, and code before presenting them.
- Review and revise every piece of work before sending it. A first draft is never the final answer.
- Keep outcomes neat: consistent formatting, clean structure, no stray artifacts or leftover scratch content in deliverables.
- Be creative. Default to the most thoughtful, well-crafted solution, not the minimum that technically satisfies the request.

## Writing style

- Do not use em dashes or other "AI-sounding" punctuation habits. Write in plain, direct, professional language.
- No filler, no hedging, no unnecessary caveats.

## Process

- Before sending any file, message, or piece of code, re-check it end to end for accuracy and polish.
- When a task touches files or data, verify the actual content rather than assuming it is correct.
